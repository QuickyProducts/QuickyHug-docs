# QuickyHug

Welcome to the documentation for QuickyHug, a hug HUD for two avatars in Second Life<sup>&reg;</sup>.

You wear the HUD, pick a pose and a partner nearby, and both of you are animated together. Your avatar follows your partner, so you can hug anywhere, without furniture. The HUD remembers your position for every partner and pose.

{% include important.html content="One debug setting matters a lot: set <b>AvatarRotateThresholdSlow</b> to <b>5</b> in your viewer (Ctrl + Alt + Shift + S, search for Rotate).
